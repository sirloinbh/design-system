## Method and limits

- Listed every non-test source file outside `components/ui/` (703 files) and compared declared function and component names across apps, then read the candidates with `git grep` and `git show`.
- Counted a candidate when it appears in two or more places: two apps, or two files of one app.
- This is a name and pattern scan, not a line-by-line read of 1,700 files. A candidate with a different name in each app can be missed, and counts are of files, not of every use. Treat "apps" and "files" below as lower bounds.
- The apps were not installed or run, and nothing in them was edited.

## 3. Proposal

Proposed to register as cards under #65, in this order:

1. **A** month helpers (S), **B** KRW format (S) and **C** `DescriptionList` (S): small, found in 3 to 6 apps.
2. **D** return-focus hook (M): found in 3 apps, and a keyboard accessibility problem each app solves differently.
3. **E** `useCopyText` (S) and **F** `useUrlParam` (M): optional, found in 2 and 4 apps.

Not proposed: **G**, **H** (two apps, different shapes; revisit when a third app needs it) and **I** (already covered by `Badge`).
